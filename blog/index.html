MAC Spoofing

Bypassing user-authentication, Aug 15, 2017

The authorisation process of logging in after connecting to Lan or Wi-Fi is done based on your Mac-address. When you log in your Mac address is sent along with the authorisation information. A table is maintained which maintains all the Mac-addresses which have been authorised. Using Mac-spoofing you can spoof the Mac address of a user who has already logged in.
